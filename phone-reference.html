Cabin & Fishing
How many people can stay in the cabin?

Cabin sleeps 2 (one queen bed). Up to 4 guests total are welcome on the property — the extra 2 can bring a tent.

What's in the cabin?
  • Queen bed
  • TV & DVD player
  • Mini-fridge
  • Front porch with rocking chairs
  • Fire pit & firewood included
  • Restroom on-site
What kind of fishing?

800 ft of private waterfront on Buffalo Creek. Spring-fed, stays cool year-round. Walk-out access right from the cabin.

TWRA stocks trout each spring. Cherokee Lake is also just minutes away (bass, crappie, catfish).

Do they need a fishing license?

Fishing rules and licensing are managed by TWRA. Tell them to check before they come:

TWRA: 1-800-332-0900  or  tn.gov/twra

Can they bring a camper or RV?

Yes. Hookup available for $30/night extra on top of cabin rate.

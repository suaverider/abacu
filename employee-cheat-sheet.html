ROOSTER RUN

Employee Quick Reference — Keep at Front Desk / Register

PRICING & PACKAGES
How much is the cabin?
$150 per night.
How many people can stay in the cabin?
Cabin sleeps 2. Up to 4 guests welcome on the property.
What comes with the cabin?
Queen bed, TV, mini-fridge, front porch, and walk-out creek access.
How much for a wedding?
$1,500 flat rate. Up to 70 guests max.
What's included with a wedding?
Ceremony area, 30x40 covered pavilion, 12x20 stage, campfire area, overnight cabin, restroom, fire pit, parking.
How much for a private event?
$500 for up to 30 guests.
$750 for up to 70 guests.
What kind of events can we host?
Birthdays, family reunions, church gatherings, cookouts, company outings — any occasion.
What's included with events?
30x40 covered pavilion, 12x20 stage, fire pit with firewood, restroom, field parking.
Is there a maximum number of guests?
70 guests max for weddings and events.
Are there any hidden fees?
No. Pricing is simple and upfront. No surprises.
THE PROPERTY & AMENITIES
Is there a restroom?
Yes. Clean, on-site unisex restroom. Not porta-potties.
How big is the pavilion?
30x40 covered pavilion with an attached 12x20 stage.
Is there a fire pit?
Yes. Fire pit with log seating. Firewood is provided.
Is there parking?
Yes. Open field parking right on the property. Room for guest vehicles and campers.
Can I bring a camper or RV?
Yes. Room to park campers on the field. RV/camper hookup available — add $30/night.
What's the creek like?
Buffalo Creek. Spring-fed, stocked by TWRA. About 800 feet of private creek frontage with walk-out access from the cabin.
Do I need a fishing license?
Check with TWRA for current fishing regulations and license requirements.
Can people visit before booking?
Call Billie or Victor to arrange a visit.
BOOKING & LOGISTICS
How do people book?
1. Call, text, or email us.
2. We check the calendar and confirm availability.
3. A deposit locks in the date.
Is a deposit required?
Yes. A deposit is required to reserve dates.
Where is Rooster Run?
351 Tampico Church Rd, Rutledge, TN 37861. Grainger County. About 45 minutes from Knoxville.
Are events weather-dependent?
Yes. All outdoor events are weather-dependent. The pavilion is covered for rain.

What else is there to do around here?
• Cherokee Lake — minutes away, bass, crappie, boating, swimming
• Panther Creek State Park — hiking, biking, overlooks
• Bean Station — restaurants, bait shops, boat ramps
 
• Grainger County Tomato Festival — every July, top 10 free festival in America

• Gatlinburg & Great Smoky Mountains — about 1 hour away

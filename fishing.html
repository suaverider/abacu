The Creek

Buffalo Creek Runs Right Through the Property

Rooster Run sits directly on Buffalo Creek in Grainger County, giving you walk-out access to the water from the moment you arrive. No long hikes to a distant river—you’re already there.

The Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency stocks this stretch of the creek with rainbow trout regularly throughout the year. The Buffalo Springs Trout Hatchery—TWRA’s largest, producing roughly 350,000 rainbow trout annually—is right next door. The spring-fed water stays a cool 58°F year-round, keeping conditions ideal for trout even in summer.

Whether you want a quiet morning solo session, a father-son weekend, or a multi-day trip with friends, you have a private base camp with a cabin, a campfire, and creek access steps from your door.

Fishing on Buffalo Creek
What You’ll Catch

Rainbow Trout, Stocked Year-Round

TWRA’s Buffalo Springs Hatchery raises about 350,000 rainbow trout every year in 24 spring-fed raceways. Those fish get released into Buffalo Creek and 30+ other water bodies across Tennessee. Being right next door means you’re fishing some of the most consistently stocked water in the state.

Stocking Schedule

When Does TWRA Stock Buffalo Creek?

Buffalo Creek is designated as a Delayed Harvest location and receives stockings throughout most of the year. Here’s the general pattern.

Winter
November – February

Peak stocking season. TWRA releases over 70,000 trout statewide during the winter program. Fish average about 10 inches. This is prime time on Buffalo Creek.

Spring
March – May

Additional spring stockings at designated locations. Warmer weather brings longer days on the creek. Water stays cool thanks to the spring-fed hatchery upstream.

Summer
June – August

The 58°F spring water keeps trout alive and active even in summer heat. A great time to combine fishing with a cabin stay and evening campfires.

Fall
September – October

Beautiful foliage along the creek. Lighter crowds and comfortable weather. Fish before the big winter stockings ramp up again.

Exact stocking dates are published on the TWRA website and may shift due to weather or water conditions. Stockings typically happen within five days of the listed date.

Important: Fishing regulations, creel limits, and stocking schedules can change at any time. The information on this page is provided for general reference only. Always verify current rules directly with TWRA at tn.gov/twra or by calling 1-800-332-0900 before you fish. Rooster Run is not responsible for outdated regulation information.
Know Before You Go

Fishing Rules & Regulations

Buffalo Creek within the Buffalo Springs WMA has special TWRA regulations. Rules can change—always check with TWRA for current regulations before you fish.

License Required

All anglers need a valid Tennessee fishing license and a trout stamp. You can purchase both online at the TWRA website or at local retailers.

Special Regulations Apply

This stretch of Buffalo Creek has special trout fishing rules set by TWRA. Creel limits, gear restrictions, and open areas can change. Check the TWRA trout page or call 1-800-332-0900 before your trip.

Wildlife Management Area

The property borders the Buffalo Springs WMA, which has its own access rules and hours. Respect posted signs and WMA boundaries during your visit.

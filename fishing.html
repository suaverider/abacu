The Creek

800 Feet of Creekside Waterfront

Rooster Run sits directly on Buffalo Creek in Grainger County with over 800 feet of private waterfront, giving you walk-out access to the water from the moment you arrive. No long hikes to a distant river—you’re already there.

Buffalo Creek is a spring-fed creek, so the water stays cool year-round—even in the warmer months. It’s a beautiful stretch of water and a great spot to spend a morning, an afternoon, or an entire weekend with a rod in hand.

Whether you want a quiet morning solo session, a father-son weekend, or a multi-day trip with friends, you have a private base camp with a cabin, a campfire, and creek access steps from your door.

What to Expect

Creek Fishing at Your Doorstep

Buffalo Creek is stocked by the Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency (TWRA), with seasonal trout fishing each spring. For details on exact dates, what’s being stocked, current regulations, and licensing requirements, contact TWRA directly—they’re the best source for up-to-date information.

  • Seasonal trout fishing each spring (check TWRA for exact dates)
  • 800 feet of private waterfront
  • Spring-fed creek stays cool year-round
  • Walk-out access right from the property
  • Cabin, campfire, and creek all in one place
  • Great for solo trips, couples, or groups
  • Contact TWRA or call 1-800-332-0900 for fishing rules and licensing info

Before You Fish

A Few Things to Know

Fishing regulations, licensing, and stocking schedules are managed by TWRA and can change at any time. We recommend checking with them before your trip.

Check with TWRA

TWRA manages fishing rules for Buffalo Creek. Visit tn.gov/twra or call 1-800-332-0900 to get the latest on licensing, regulations, and what’s being stocked.

Also Nearby

Cherokee Lake—Minutes Away

If you want to mix creek fishing with lake fishing, Cherokee Lake is just a short drive from the property. It’s a popular spot for bass, crappie, catfish, and more.

Stay the Night

A Cozy Sleeper Cabin on the Creek

After a day on the water, you don’t have to drive home. The on-site sleeper cabin is small but comfortable—just enough room for a queen bed, a TV on the wall, and a mini-fridge to keep your drinks cold. It’s not a suite, it’s a place to crash after a good day of fishing.

The front porch faces away from the creek for a little privacy, with rocking chairs where you can sit and unwind. But open the window inside and Buffalo Creek is right there—close enough to hear the water while you fall asleep.

Book the cabin for an overnight fishing trip and you’ve got everything in one spot: creek access, a campfire, and a bed for the night. Full amenities included—bathroom, pavilion access, fire pit, and picnic tables.

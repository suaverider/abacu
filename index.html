4 Training Phases Per Session

1Warmup

Addition and subtraction drills. Work the problems on your physical soroban and type the answer to check.

2Bead Reading

See a soroban bead pattern, type the number it represents. Trains instant recognition of bead positions.

3Flash Anzan

Numbers flash on screen — calculate the running total mentally. Difficulty adapts automatically to your performance.

4Complements

Quick-fire complement drills. Friends of 10, then Friends of 5. Essential for efficient bead technique.

FAQ
Do I need a physical soroban?▼
Recommended for Phase 1 (Warmup) but not required. Phases 2-4 are screen-only exercises.
How long should I train daily?▼
20 minutes is the recommended starting point. Consistency matters more than duration — daily 20-minute sessions beat occasional long ones.
Does the online version save my progress?▼
Yes. Progress saves to your browser's localStorage on the same domain. The downloaded version saves separately in your local browser.
Is this for beginners?▼
Yes. The warmup phase starts with simple 2-3 digit problems, and the flash anzan phase begins at Easy level (two single-digit numbers, 3-second display). Difficulty adapts to your skill level automatically.
Can I use this on my phone?▼
Yes. The online version works on any phone or tablet browser. For the downloaded file, open it in your mobile browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox). The interface adapts to smaller screens.
What browsers are supported?▼
Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari — any modern browser on desktop or mobile.
Is this free?▼
Yes, Soroban Trainer Pro is free for personal use.

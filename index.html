The Area

Just 45 Minutes from Knoxville—A World Away

Rooster Run sits in the heart of Grainger County, one of East Tennessee’s most beautiful rural landscapes. Here’s what’s nearby.

Cherokee Lake

Nearly 30,000 acres and 400 miles of shoreline—minutes away. Bass, crappie, walleye, catfish, boating, and swimming.

Buffalo Creek Falls

A scenic waterfall right on Buffalo Creek. A beautiful spot to explore and fish along the water.

Panther Creek State Park

Hiking and mountain biking trails on Cherokee Lake with mountaintop overlooks.

Grainger County Tomato Festival

One of the top 10 festivals in America. 30,000 people come every July for the county’s famous tomatoes, live music, and Southern charm.

Great Smoky Mountains

Gatlinburg and the Smokies are about 75 miles south—easy day trip distance.

Small-Town Tennessee

Rutledge is a quiet county seat of about 1,300 people. Quilt trails on barns, local shops, and genuine Southern hospitality.

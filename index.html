FAQ
Do I need a physical soroban (abacus)?▼
Phase 1 (Warmup) is designed to be worked on a physical soroban — the app shows you problems and you solve them on your abacus, then type the answer to check. Phases 2-4 are screen-only and don't require a physical abacus.
What do I get when I buy?▼
You get a single HTML file that you download and keep forever. Double-click it to open in your browser. No internet needed, no accounts, no subscriptions. It's yours.
How long should I train daily?▼
20 minutes is the recommended starting point. Consistency matters more than duration — daily 20-minute sessions beat occasional long ones.
Does it save my scores?▼
Yes. The full version saves your session history and daily streak in your browser. Your data stays on your device — nothing is sent anywhere. The free demo does not save history.
Is this for beginners?▼
Yes. The warmup starts with simple 2-3 digit problems, and flash anzan begins at Easy level (two single-digit numbers). Difficulty adapts to your skill level automatically.
Can I use this on my phone?▼
Yes. The demo runs in any phone or tablet browser. The downloaded file also works on mobile — open it in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox.

Fishing

Buffalo Creek Runs Right Through the Property

Rooster Run sits directly on Buffalo Creek in Grainger County, giving you walk-out access to the water from the moment you arrive. The Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency (TWRA) stocks this stretch of the creek with trout, so you can fish without a long drive to some distant river.

Whether you want a quiet morning solo session, a father-son weekend, or a multi-day trip with friends, you have a private base camp with a cabin, a campfire, and creek access steps from your door.

  • Direct walk-out access to Buffalo Creek
  • TWRA-stocked trout in the creek
  • Adjacent to the 342-acre Buffalo Springs WMA
  • Day trips or overnight cabin stays
  • Campfire area for evening gatherings
  • Valid Tennessee fishing license required

4 Training Phases Per Session

1Warmup

Addition and subtraction drills on your physical soroban. Build speed and accuracy with real bead movement.

2Bead Reading

See a soroban bead pattern, type the number it represents. Trains instant recognition of bead positions.

3Anzan

Numbers flash on screen — add them in your head. Difficulty adapts automatically to your performance.

4Complements

Quick-fire complement drills. Friends of 10, then Friends of 5. Essential for efficient bead technique.

FAQ
Do I need a physical soroban?▼
Recommended for Phase 1 (Warmup) but not required. Phases 2-4 are screen-only exercises.
How long should I train daily?▼
20 minutes is the recommended starting point. Consistency matters more than duration — daily 20-minute sessions beat occasional long ones.
Does the online version save my progress?▼
Yes. Progress saves to your browser's localStorage on the same domain. The downloaded version saves separately in your local browser.
What browsers are supported?▼
Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. Any modern browser that supports ES6 and localStorage.
Is this free?▼
Yes, Soroban Trainer Pro is free for personal use.

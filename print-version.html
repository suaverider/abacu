The Area — Just 45 Minutes from Knoxville

  • Cherokee Lake — A popular lake just minutes away. Great for bass, crappie, catfish, boating, and swimming.
  • Bean Station & Local Dining — Local restaurants, bait shops, and boat ramps on Cherokee Lake.
  • Panther Creek State Park — Hiking and mountain biking trails on Cherokee Lake with mountaintop overlooks.
  • Grainger County Tomato Festival — Named one of the top 10 free festivals in America. Tens of thousands come every July.
  • Dollywood & Pigeon Forge — About an hour from the property. Spend a day at Dollywood, then come back to the peace and quiet of the creek.
  • Small-Town Tennessee — Rutledge is a quiet county seat of about 1,300 people. Quilt trails on barns, local shops, and genuine Southern hospitality.

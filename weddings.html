Rooster Run is for couples who want something intimate and real—not a big production in a banquet hall. A country wedding on the creek with close family and friends, where the setting does the decorating for you.

Exchange vows along Buffalo Creek. Have your reception under the covered pavilion. End the night around the campfire with the people who matter most. No stuffy formality, no filler—just a beautiful day in a beautiful country setting.

We keep things small on purpose—up to 70 guests, $1,500 flat. This is the kind of place where you can have the wedding you actually want, without the price tag of a big venue.

What’s Included

Your Wedding at Rooster Run

Everything you need for a beautiful outdoor ceremony and celebration.

Creekside Ceremony Area

Exchange vows along Buffalo Creek with the sound of water as your backdrop. Natural beauty that photographs beautifully without expensive decorating.

30×40 Covered Pavilion

A spacious 30×40 covered pavilion to host your reception, dinner, and dancing—rain or shine. An attached 12×20 stage with roof is ready for your DJ or live band.

Drive right onto the property and park on the grass. Plenty of room for your guests—no overflow parking concerns.

Picture Your Day

How It All Comes Together

Morning

Arrive & Settle In

Get to the property early and take your time. The on-site restroom under the pavilion has a sink, mirror, and cabinets—plenty of room to freshen up. Grab coffee, take a walk along the creek, and soak it in before the big moment.

Afternoon

Ceremony by the Creek

Guests gather along Buffalo Creek for the ceremony. Trees overhead, water at your feet, open sky above. Small enough that everyone can hear, big enough to feel like a celebration.

Evening

Reception Under the Pavilion

Dinner, toasts, and dancing under the covered pavilion. String your own lights, bring your own caterer and DJ. Make it yours.

Night

Campfire Under the Stars

The best part of the night. Gather around the fire, tell stories, and let the day sink in. Fireflies in summer. Stars year-round.

Common Questions

How many guests can you accommodate?

Up to 70 guests. The 30×40 covered pavilion gives you plenty of room for tables, a dance floor, and everyone you want there. It’s the perfect size for an intimate country wedding.

Can we bring our own caterer and DJ?

Absolutely. There are no required vendor lists. Bring whoever you want for food, music, photography, flowers—everything.

What about bad weather?

The covered pavilion keeps your reception dry no matter what. For ceremonies, many couples set up under or near the pavilion as a rain backup.

How far is it from Knoxville?

About 45 minutes northeast of Knoxville. Close enough for guests to get here easily, far enough to feel like a getaway.

What does it cost?

$1,500 for up to 70 guests. That includes the creekside ceremony area, covered pavilion, campfire, overnight cabin for the couple, restroom, and on-site parking. Call or text Billie at 423-736-8821 or Victor at 423-736-8820 to check availability.
